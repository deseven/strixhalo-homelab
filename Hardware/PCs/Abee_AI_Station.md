# Abee AI Station

![Abee AI Station](./abee_ai_station.jpg)

[[OFFICIAL PRODUCT PAGE](https://www.abeepc.com/BarebonePCs/166.html)]

High-end mini PC featuring the AMD Ryzen AI+ Max 395 APU with advanced liquid cooling solution. The Abee AI Station differentiates itself with an in-house custom liquid cooling system featuring dual 92 mm fans and a large cold plate covering most of the motherboard.

## Specifications

- **Processor:** AMD Ryzen AI+ Max 395 APU
- **iGPU:** AMD Radeon 8060S
- **RAM:** 128 GB (LPDDR5X)
- **Storage:** 2 TB M.2 SSD
- **Cooling:** Custom liquid cooling with dual 92 mm fans
- **Power Supply:** 400 W internal PSU
- **TDP:** Up to 176 W (APU under full load)
- **Noise Level:** ~35.1 dB(A) under full load
- **Dimensions:** 199 x 199 x 199 mm
- **Weight:** 5.3 kg
- **Chassis:** All-aluminium construction

## Availability

Released in China (November 2025). Global availability has not been announced.
