# Aoostar NEX395

![Aoostar NEX395](./aoostar-nex395.jpg)

[[OFFICIAL PRODUCT PAGE](https://aoostar.com/)] - Unavailable

Aoostar's entry into the AMD Strix Halo mini-PC market. Features an unassuming design compared to other Strix Halo mini-PCs, with dual 10G Ethernet and integrated PSU. Includes "Grandmaster Posture" feature (functionality unclear).

Availability: 2025 in China, global availability confirmed.

> [!NOTE]
> The Aoostar NEX395 is positioned as a more compact alternative to competing Strix Halo mini-PCs like the GMKtec EVO-X2 and Minisforum MS-S1 Max.

### Specifications

- **Processor**: AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395 (16 Zen 5 CPU cores)
- **GPU**: Radeon 8060S iGPU
- **RAM**: 64GB or 128GB LPDDR5X
- **Storage**: 1TB NVMe SSD (configurable)
- **Networking**: Dual 10G Ethernet
- **Ports**: Dual Ethernet + 5 USB ports on rear, additional I/O on front
- **Power Supply**: Integrated PSU
- **Design**: Mac Studio-style compact chassis

### Pricing

- China: ~$2,100+ (at 2025 exchange rates)
- Global pricing: To be announced
