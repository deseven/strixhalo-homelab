# MSI AI Edge

[![MSI AI Edge](./msi-ai-edge.jpg?thumbnail=400)](./msi-ai-edge.jpg)

[[MSI Launches AI Edge Series Desktop, Pioneering the Next Era of Edge AI Computing](https://www.msi.com/news/detail/MSI-Launches-AI-Edge-Series-Desktop--Pioneering-the-Next-Era-of-Edge-AI-Computing-147555)]

MSI's second AI-branded mini-PC / compact desktop, announced in January 2026 and positioned for local AI inference, privacy-sensitive workloads, and creator use. The launch hardware centers on AMD's Strix Halo platform, with MSI highlighting the Ryzen AI Max+ 395 configuration.

### Specifications

- **Launch timing:** January 2026
- **Form factor:** 4-liter compact desktop with a built-in power supply
- **Processor:** AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395
- **Memory:** Up to 128 GB LPDDR5X-8000 unified memory
- **Graphics memory:** Up to 96 GB dynamically assignable to the GPU
- **iGPU:** Radeon 8060S with 40 compute units
- **NPU:** XDNA 2, rated at up to 50 TOPS
- **AI performance:** MSI claims up to 126 total TOPS
- **LLM claim:** About 15 TPS on models up to 120B parameters
- **Cooling:** Glacier Armor thermal solution
- **Operating system:** Windows or Linux

### Platform

Built on the [[Sixunited AXB35 board|Hardware/Boards/Sixunited_AXB35]] platform.

### Notes

MSI has not announced pricing or final availability at the time of the launch article. MSI may eventually offer lower-tier Ryzen AI Max variants, but the company has only publicly shown the Ryzen AI Max+ 395 model so far.
