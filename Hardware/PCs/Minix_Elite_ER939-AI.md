# Minix ER939-AI

![Minix ER939-AI](./minix-er939-ai.jpg)

[[OFFICIAL PRODUCT PAGE](https://www.minix.com.hk/products/minix-er939-ai-amd-ryzen-ai-max-395-mini-pc-eu)] - currently redirects into a Shop app session, so public pricing and availability are not clearly published.

Minix's entry into the AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395 mini-workstation market. It is built around the same high-end Strix Halo APU class as other performance-oriented mini PCs, but adds a more workstation-focused feature set.

Availability: not announced yet.

### Specifications

- **Processor / APU**: AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395
- **CPU cores**: 16 Zen 5 cores
- **Graphics**: Radeon 8060S integrated graphics
- **AI**: 50 TOPS NPU, 126 TOPS total claimed AI performance
- **Memory**: up to 128 GB LPDDR5 at 8000 MHz
- **Storage**: 2 TB M.2 SSD, plus an additional M.2 slot
- **Networking**: Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.4, 2.5 GbE
- **Video output**: HDMI, DisplayPort, USB4 on the front and rear
- **Other features**: memory card slot, fingerprint sensor
- **Cooling**: three individual fans
- **Dimensions**: 205 x 192 x 70 mm
