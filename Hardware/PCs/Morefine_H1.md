# MOREFINE H1

![MOREFINE H1](./morefine_h1.jpg)

[[OFFICIAL PRODUCT PAGE](https://www.morefine.com/product/morefine-h1-amd-ryzen-ai-max-pro-395-mini-pc)]

MOREFINE's flagship Strix Halo mini PC. The H1 ships with AMD Ryzen AI Max+ PRO 395, 128GB of LPDDR5x memory, and a 2TB SSD. It is positioned as a compact workstation-class system for AI and other heavy workloads.

Availability: announced and listed for sale in late 2025.

## Specifications

- **Processor**: AMD Ryzen AI Max+ PRO 395
- **GPU**: Radeon 8060S iGPU
- **NPU**: 50 TOPS
- **Memory**: 128GB LPDDR5x at 8000MHz
- **Storage**: 2TB SSD, with room for a second M.2 2280 SSD
- **Networking**: 2.5GbE Ethernet, WiFi 7, Bluetooth 5.4
- **Video outputs**: 1x HDMI 2.1, 1x DisplayPort 1.4, 2x USB4
- **Other ports**: 3x USB 3.2 Gen 2, front SD card slot
- **Power supply**: 350W PSU
- **Dimensions**: about 9.8 x 7.4 x 3.8 inches
- **Operating system**: Windows 11 Pro

## Platform

Built on the [[Sixunited AXB35 board|Hardware/Boards/Sixunited_AXB35]] platform.
